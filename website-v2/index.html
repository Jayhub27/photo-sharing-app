How it works

Three steps from camera roll to paid.

  1. Make a collection

    Name it and add photos from your phone, or paste links. Thumbnails and sizes are generated for you.

  2. Send the QR code

    Share the link or let people scan the code. No install and no account needed to look.

  3. Get paid

    Set a price per collection. Stripe handles the card, and every original unlocks on payment.

Questions

The short answers.

Is it really free?

Creating, uploading and sharing cost nothing. You only meet money if you price a collection, and then it's Stripe's usual card fee.

Do people need the app to see my photos?

No. A link or a QR code opens the gallery in any phone browser. The app is for shooting, uploading and managing.

What happens after someone pays?

Stripe confirms the checkout through a webhook, the purchase is recorded, and every original in that collection unlocks for that buyer, ZIP downloads included.

Which files can I import?

Photos from your device, direct image links, Google Drive file links, and any page with an og:image tag. Up to 10 links at a time, 25 MB per image.

Is my data safe?

Passwords are bcrypt hashed, sessions are HTTP-only cookies, and the storage bucket is private: images stream through authenticated endpoints only. Row level security is on.

Can I run it myself?

Yes. The source includes the mobile app, the API and the database schema. Bring your own Supabase project and Stripe keys.
