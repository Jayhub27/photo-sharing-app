What buyers see, and when files unlock
  • Anyone with the link sees the 640px thumbnails. No account, no app.
  • Originals, single downloads and ZIPs stay locked until payment.
  • Checkout is Stripe's own page: card, Apple Pay or Google Pay.
  • The webhook records the purchase, and the originals unlock for that buyer on any device.
  • Owners and invited editors always have full access.

Private by default

Your server, your photos, no trackers.

  • Your storage, not ours

    A private bucket, streamed through an access check.

  • No trackers

    No analytics, pixels, ads or third-party scripts.

  • Buyers stay anonymous

    No account, and their email only goes to Stripe.

  • Deletes on a schedule

    1 to 3650 days, storage included.

What the server actually stores
  • Accounts: name, email and a bcrypt password hash.
  • Photos and metadata, in a private bucket — originals only ever stream through an access check.
  • Sessions as HTTP-only cookies, collection membership, and purchase records from Stripe.
  • Nothing else. No analytics, no third-party fonts or scripts, no ad tech.
  • Scheduled deletion removes the storage objects, photo rows, members and purchase records within the hour.

Questions

The short answers.

Is it really free?

Creating, uploading and sharing cost nothing. You only meet money if you price a collection — then it's Stripe's card fee.

Do people need the app to see my photos?

No. A link or QR code opens the gallery in any phone browser.

What happens after someone pays?

Stripe confirms the checkout, the purchase is recorded, and every original unlocks for that buyer — ZIPs included.

Which files can I import?

Your device's photos, direct image links, Google Drive links, or any page with an og:image tag. Ten at a time, 25 MB each.

Is my data safe?

Private bucket, bcrypt password hashes, HTTP-only sessions, and row level security on every table.

Can I run it myself?

Yes. Bring your own Supabase project and Stripe keys.
