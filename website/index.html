What it does

No fluff. Six things, done right.

01

Collections

Group shots into named collections. Grid or list, search, sort and filter that stick in the browser.

02

QR sharing

Every collection gets a link and a QR code. Scan it and the gallery opens in any phone browser.

03

Sell originals

Set a price per collection. Buyers pay with a card through Stripe Checkout and unlock instantly.

04

Locked until paid

Priced collections stream 640px thumbnails only. Originals, single downloads and ZIPs stay locked.

05

Import anywhere

Device picker, direct image URLs, Google Drive links, or any page with an og:image tag.

06

Collaborate

Invite viewers and editors by email. New photos appear for everyone within about 5 seconds.

How it works

Three steps. That is the whole thing.

  1. Step 1

    Make a collection

    Name it, add photos from your phone or paste links. Thumbnails are generated automatically.

  2. Step 2

    Share the QR

  3. Step 3

    Get paid

    Set a price or keep it free. Stripe handles checkout, downloads unlock on payment.

Questions

Short answers, no fluff.

Is it really free?

Yes. Creating collections, uploading and sharing are free. You only touch money if you decide to sell a collection, and then Stripe takes its usual card fee.

Do people need the app to view my photos?

No. A collection link or QR code opens in any phone browser. The app is for shooting, uploading and managing.

What happens after someone pays?

Stripe confirms the checkout through a webhook, the purchase is recorded, and every original file in that collection unlocks for that buyer, including ZIP downloads.

Which files can I import?

Photos from your device, direct image URLs, Google Drive file links, and any web page with an og:image tag. Max 10 links per request, 25 MB per image.

Is my data safe?

Passwords are bcrypt hashed, sessions are HTTP-only cookies, the storage bucket is private and images stream through authenticated endpoints. Supabase RLS is enabled on every table.

Can I run it myself?

Yes. The source zip has the Expo app, the Express API and the Supabase schema. Bring your own Supabase project and Stripe keys.
