What it does

No fluff. Six things, done right.

Collections

Group shots into named collections. Grid or list, search, sort and filter that stick in the browser.

QR sharing

Every collection gets a link and a QR code. Scan it and the gallery opens in any phone browser.

Sell originals

Set a price per collection. Buyers pay with a card through Stripe Checkout and unlock instantly.

Locked until paid

Priced collections stream 640px thumbnails only. Originals, single downloads and ZIPs stay locked.

Import anywhere

Device picker, direct image URLs, Google Drive links, or any page with an og:image tag.

Collaborate

Invite viewers and editors by email. New photos appear for everyone within about 5 seconds.

Why Take the shot

Private, simple, and no subscription

Private by design

Self-host it and your photos never leave your server. No analytics, no tracking pixels, no ads, no third-party scripts or fonts. The storage bucket is private and every image streams through an access check.

Simple by default

Name a collection, add photos, share the QR. Buyers just open a link: no app install, no account, no checkout maze. One price per collection instead of plan tiers and feature gates.

An alternative to subscriptions

Other galleries charge $8-50 per month and keep a commission on sales. Run this yourself and you pay your own Stripe fees only. Free to share, and it can delete itself after 1-3650 days.

How it works

Three steps. That is the whole thing.

  1. Step 1 01

    Make a collection

    Name it, add photos from your phone or paste links. Thumbnails are generated automatically.

  2. Step 2 02

    Share the QR

    Send the link or let people scan the QR code. No install required to view or buy.

  3. Step 3 03

    Get paid

    Set a price or keep it free. Stripe handles checkout, downloads unlock on payment.

Questions

Short answers, no fluff.

Is it really free?

Yes. Creating collections, uploading and sharing cost nothing. The only costs are your own hosting and Stripe's usual card fee, and only if you decide to sell.

Do you track me or the people who view my galleries?

No. There is no analytics, no tracking pixel, no advertising and no third-party script or font. Everything - including the app's own assets - is served from your own server.

Where do my photos live?

On your own storage. The bucket is private and every image streams through an authenticated endpoint after an access check. Passwords are bcrypt hashed, sessions are HTTP-only cookies, and Supabase RLS is enabled on every table.

Do people need the app to view my photos?

No. A collection link or QR code opens in any phone browser. The app is for shooting, uploading and managing.

How do I get paid?

Add your Stripe keys to the server. Buyers pay by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay through Stripe Checkout, and the money lands in your Stripe account - Stripe pays out to your bank on your normal schedule. Running an instance for several photographers? Set their Stripe Connect account and the payment is transferred straight to them.

What does selling cost?

0% commission. There is no subscription and no per-sale cut; you only pay Stripe's card fee. If you run a multi-photographer instance you can optionally keep a platform fee for yourself.

What happens after someone pays?

Stripe confirms the checkout through a webhook, the purchase is recorded, and every original file in that collection unlocks for that buyer, including ZIP downloads.

Can I run it myself?

Yes. It is MIT licensed: bring your own Supabase project and Stripe keys, or docker compose up. The full walkthrough is in the getting started guide.

Which files can I import?

Photos from your device, direct image URLs, Google Drive file links, and any web page with an og:image tag. Max 10 links per request, 25 MB per image.
