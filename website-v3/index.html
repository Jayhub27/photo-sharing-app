Questions.

The short version.

Is it really free?

Sharing is free. Selling costs only Stripe's card fee.

Is there a subscription?

No. Nothing to cancel.

Do people need the app to see my photos?

No. A link opens in any browser.

What happens after someone pays?

Stripe confirms, then the originals unlock for that buyer.

Which files can I import?

Device photos, direct links, Google Drive, or any page with og:image. 25 MB each.

Is my data safe?

Private bucket, bcrypt hashes, HTTP-only sessions, row-level security.

Can I run it myself?

Yes. Bring your own Supabase project and Stripe keys.
