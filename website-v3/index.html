Example collections, placeholder prices. The app, the lock and the checkout are real.

What buyers see, and when files unlock
  • Anyone with the link sees the 640px previews. No account, no app.
  • Originals, single downloads and ZIPs stay locked until payment.
  • Checkout is Stripe's own page: card, Apple Pay or Google Pay.
  • The webhook records the purchase, and the originals unlock for that buyer on any device.
  • Owners and invited editors always have full access.

Private by default.

A private bucket, an access check on every byte, and a delete date you choose.

  • Your storage, not ours

    Photos live in your own Supabase bucket. The server streams each file only after an access check on the collection.

  • No trackers

    No analytics, pixels, ads or third-party fonts. This page loads only from its own origin.

  • Buyers stay anonymous

    No account needed. Their email is passed to Stripe for the receipt and nothing else.

  • It deletes itself

    Pick 1 to 3650 days. The hourly sweeper removes files, photo rows, members and purchase records.

What the server actually stores
  • Accounts: name, email and a bcrypt password hash.
  • Photos and metadata, in a private bucket — originals only ever stream through an access check.
  • Sessions as HTTP-only cookies, collection membership, and purchase records from Stripe.
  • Nothing else. No analytics, no third-party fonts or scripts, no ad tech.

Questions.

The short answers. The longer ones live in the repo.

Is it really free?

Creating, uploading and sharing cost nothing. You only meet money if you price a collection — then it is Stripe's card fee.

Is there a subscription?

No. There is no plan, no seat count and nothing to cancel.

Do people need the app to see my photos?

No. A link or QR code opens the gallery in any phone browser.

What happens after someone pays?

Stripe confirms the checkout, the purchase is recorded, and every original unlocks for that buyer — ZIPs included.

Which files can I import?

Your device's photos, direct image links, Google Drive links, or any page with an og:image tag. Ten at a time, 25 MB each.

Is my data safe?

A private bucket, bcrypt password hashes, HTTP-only sessions, and row level security on every table.

Can I run it myself?

Yes. Bring your own Supabase project and Stripe keys. The repo has the schema, the environment list and a setup guide.
