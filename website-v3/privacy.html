Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Take the shot is a photo-sharing app you can run yourself. This policy covers the hosted landing site and the hosted web app at take-the-shot-api.vercel.app. If you deploy your own copy, the operator of that copy decides what it stores and is responsible for it.

The landing site

The landing site is plain HTML, CSS and one small script served from your own origin. It has no analytics, no tracking pixels, no advertising cookies and no third-party fonts or scripts. Fonts and images come from the same domain.

What the web app stores

  • Account: your name, email address and a bcrypt password hash. Passwords are never stored in plain text.
  • Photos and collections: the files you upload, generated thumbnails, dimensions, filenames, collection names, prices and expiry dates.
  • Sessions: a random session token in an HTTP-only cookie, plus its creation time.
  • Purchases: buyer email, amount, currency, status and Stripe identifiers. Card details go directly to Stripe and never reach the app server.
  • Feedback: the message you send through the feedback box, the page it came from and basic display settings.
  • Errors: if the operator configures Sentry or an alert webhook, error details may be sent there.

What is never collected

  • No advertising identifiers, tracking pixels or third-party analytics by default.
  • No precise location, contacts or device advertising ID.
  • No card numbers or bank details.

Cookies and local storage

After login the app sets one HTTP-only session cookie. The browser may also store theme, view, onboarding and background preferences in local storage. There are no advertising or cross-site cookies.

Third parties

Depending on how the service is deployed, data may be processed by:

  • Supabase for the database and private object storage.
  • Stripe for checkout, receipts and seller payouts.
  • Vercel or another host for serving the app.
  • Sentry or an alert webhook, only when configured.

Each provider processes data under its own terms.

Retention and deletion

  • Collections can be set to auto-delete after 1 to 3650 days. The sweeper removes the photos, members and purchase records.
  • You can delete individual photos or a whole collection at any time.
  • To delete an account, contact the operator through the project issue tracker.
  • Some purchase records may be kept where tax or accounting rules require it.

Security

Photos live in a private storage bucket and every image byte is streamed through an access check. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt, sessions are HTTP-only, and production traffic uses HTTPS. No system is perfect; please report a problem through the issue tracker instead of testing it against other people’s collections.

Children

The service is not intended for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue on GitHub.
